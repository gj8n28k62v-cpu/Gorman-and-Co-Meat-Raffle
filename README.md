# Meat Raffle

Draws the winning numbers for the Gorman and Co Thursday meat raffle and shows them on a big screen.

## Opening the app

**Windows:** Unzip `Meat Raffle-1.0-windows.zip`, open the `Meat Raffle` folder and double-click **Meat Raffle.exe**.
The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Keep everything in the folder together: the app won't start if `Meat Raffle.exe` is moved away from the `app` and `runtime` folders. For a desktop icon, right-click `Meat Raffle.exe` and choose **Send to > Desktop (create shortcut)**.

**Mac:** Open `Meat Raffle-1.0.dmg` and drag Meat Raffle into Applications. If macOS won't open it, go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

Two windows open:

- **Meat Raffle** is the display for the room. It shows the logo until the first number is drawn.
- **Raffle Controls** is for the person running the raffle in the office.

## Running a raffle

1. Drag the **Meat Raffle** window onto the TV and click **Full Screen** in the controls. Keep the controls on your own screen.
2. Type the lowest ticket number and click the **SUBMIT** button under it.
3. Type the highest ticket number and click the **SUBMIT** button under it.
4. Click **Generate Number**. Numbers spin for a few seconds, then the winning number appears. A moment later it is added to the **Previous Draws** list.
5. Click **Generate Number** again for each prize. A number that has already been drawn is never drawn again.

## Good to know

- Typing a number isn't enough: click its **SUBMIT** button. Use whole numbers only.
- If nothing happens when you click **Generate Number**, check that both numbers were submitted and that the lowest isn't higher than the highest.
- **Previous Draws** holds the first six draws. Later winning numbers still appear on the big display, but aren't added to the list.
- Don't click **Generate Number** once every number in the range has been drawn, or the app will freeze. If it does, close it (Windows may ask you to **Close the program**) and open it again.
- To start a new raffle, close both windows and open the app again. This clears the previous draws and makes every number available again.
